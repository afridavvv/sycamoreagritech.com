关于我们About us

扎根产地，稳健长久Deep roots, steady growth

Sycamore（梧桐）是一种根深叶茂的树。我们以此为名，希望像它一样稳健、长久：与产地建立长期合作，为客户提供值得信赖的货源。 The sycamore is a broad, deep-rooted tree. We took its name because that is the kind of business we want to be: steady and long-lived, built on lasting relationships with growers and dependable supply for our customers.

我们熟悉农产品的种植周期、质量标准与贸易流程。告诉我们您需要的品类、规格、数量和交期，我们会匹配合适的产地与供应商，并全程跟进直至货物交付。 We know crop cycles, quality standards and trade procedures. Tell us the product, specification, volume and timing you need, and we match you with the right origin and supplier, then follow the order all the way to delivery.

“栽下梧桐树，引得凤凰来。”

“Plant a sycamore, and the phoenix will come.”

做好扎实的事，好的伙伴自然会来。An old Chinese saying: build something worthwhile, and good partners follow.

合作流程How we work

五步完成一笔订单Five steps from inquiry to delivery

  1. 01

    需求沟通Tell us what you need

    确认品类、规格、数量、交期与目的地。Product, spec, volume, timing and destination.

  2. 02

    寻源报价Sourcing & quote

    匹配合适的产地与供应商，提供样品与报价。We match origins and suppliers, then send samples and a quote.

  3. 03

    验货质检Inspection

    装运前检验品质与规格，留存检验记录。Quality and specs are checked before shipment, with records kept.

  4. 04

    物流与单证Logistics & documents

    安排仓储、运输、报关及相关证书。Warehousing, freight, customs and certificates arranged.

  5. 05

    交付与跟进Delivery & follow-up

    按时交付，并跟进到货反馈。On-time delivery, and we follow up after arrival.

品质保障Quality

品质与合规，放在首位Quality and compliance come first

农产品贸易中，品质与单证决定了一笔订单能否顺利完成。我们把这两件事放在首位。In agricultural trade, quality and paperwork decide whether an order goes smoothly. We put both first.

  • 来源可追溯Traceable origin

    每批货物记录产地、批次与供应商信息。Every lot is recorded with its origin, batch and supplier.

  • 检验有据可查Documented inspection

    按合同标准检验，可安排独立第三方机构出具报告。Inspected to contract standards, with independent third-party reports available.

  • 单证合规Compliant paperwork

    协助准备植物检疫证书、原产地证明、质检报告等文件。We help prepare phytosanitary certificates, certificates of origin, quality reports and more.
